# Product updates

## 6 November 2026

PagePing has a new look. The local development preview now has a new brand, logo, website and app design:

- A new logo and wordmark, with light and dark versions.
- A redesigned landing page with an automatic walkthrough that shows a watch being set up and a price drop being caught, using an illustrative demonstration shop. It pauses when scrolled away and respects reduced-motion settings.
- A redesigned app: watch evidence, Watch Collections, groups, notifications, check schedule, import and export, and help pages.
- Clearer confirmations and information messages, and keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Friendlier handling when the service can't be reached.

Checks covered sign-up, setting up a watch, detecting a change, collections and alerts in a browser, plus real page fetches in both HTTP and browser modes, in light and dark themes at phone and desktop widths. These are local verification results; PagePing has not launched publicly.

A [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) now shows what's done and what comes next.

## 4 November 2026

The local development preview now has clearer account navigation and better small-screen access:

- Sign in opens the login form directly.
- Password reset has a direct route back to sign in.
- Registration controls reflect whether new accounts are currently accepted.
- The mobile header keeps sign-in accessible, including on narrow screens.
- Dialogs have accessible titles, and hidden mobile navigation links no longer receive keyboard focus.
- Checkout previews clearly identify test-only payments.

Browser checks covered login, registration, password reset and layouts at phone, tablet and desktop widths. These are local verification results; PagePing has not launched publicly.
