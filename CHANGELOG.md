# Product updates

## 4 November 2026

The local development preview now has clearer account navigation and better small-screen access:

- Sign in opens the login form directly.
- Password reset has a direct route back to sign in.
- Registration controls reflect whether new accounts are currently accepted.
- The mobile header keeps sign-in accessible, including on narrow screens.
- Dialogs have accessible titles, and hidden mobile navigation links no longer receive keyboard focus.
- Checkout previews clearly identify test-only payments.

Browser checks covered login, registration, password reset and layouts at phone, tablet and desktop widths. These are local verification results; PagePing has not launched publicly.
