# Frequently asked questions

## Can I use PagePing publicly yet?

Not yet. PagePing is currently a local development preview. This repository will be updated when a public version is available.

## What does PagePing monitor?

The current build monitors selected content on supported public webpages, with conditions for changes such as phrases, numbers, prices and stock status. Support varies by website.

## Are the examples real shops or live results?

The example shop and price-drop walkthrough are illustrative demonstrations. They do not represent a live merchant or a guarantee of a particular monitoring result.

## Is PagePing open source?

The application source is private. This public repository is a presentation of the product and its progress.

## Can I buy a subscription?

Live payments are not available. Pricing and allowances shown in previews are proposals and remain subject to validation.

## Does PagePing make automatic purchases?

No. The current build focuses on monitoring and evidence. It does not make purchases for you.

## Does it work on every website?

No. Some websites block automated access, and page structure and access requirements can affect monitoring.
